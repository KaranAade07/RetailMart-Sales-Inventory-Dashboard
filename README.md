# RetailMart-Sales-Inventory-Dashboard
Interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze retail sales, inventory performance, supplier contribution, customer behavior, and business insights using Power BI.


## 📌 Project Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of retail sales, inventory levels, supplier performance, customer behavior, and product trends. It enables business users to monitor key performance indicators and make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

RetailMart management wants to:

- Monitor overall sales performance
- Track inventory levels
- Identify low-stock products
- Compare supplier performance
- Analyze city-wise sales
- Identify top-selling products
- Understand customer purchasing behavior

---

## 📊 KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Total Profit
- Average Order Value
- Profit Margin %

---

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### Executive Dashboard

- Sales Overview
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Sales by City
- KPI Cards

### Inventory Dashboard

- Current Inventory
- Low Stock Products
- Warehouse Analysis
- Supplier Performance

### Customer Dashboard

- Customer Distribution
- Top Customers
- Customer Sales Analysis

### Product Dashboard

- Top Products
- Bottom Products
- Product Profit Analysis

---

## 💡 Business Insights

- Electronics generated the highest revenue.
- Mumbai recorded the highest sales.
- Multiple products require immediate restocking.
- Supplier performance varies across product categories.
- A small group of customers contributes significantly to overall sales.
